01
शरीर

Body Discipline

Anything which promotes movement — strength training, endurance, yoga, aerobics. शरीर is your most sacred instrument; treat it accordingly.

ShariraMovementStrength
02
आहार

Nutrition

Mindful eating, balancing macros, staying hydrated. Real food = Real energy. आहार is medicine when chosen wisely — dal, sabzi, and ghee included.

AahaarMindful EatingBalance
03
मानस

Mental Toughness

Meditation, breathwork, journaling. Mental resilience > Digital chaos. A strong मानस is the foundation of every achievement worth having.

ManasMeditationResilience
04
निद्रा

Rest & Recovery

Restorative sleep, active recovery, mobility. Good rest = Better mood, strength and healthspan. निद्रा is not laziness — it is sacred medicine.

NidraSleepRecovery
05
समाज

Community

Building good relations, socialising. Growing together — Milke Badho. समाज reminds us we are far stronger inside a tribe than standing alone.

SamaajCommunityConnection
06
प्रकृति

Nature

Aligning with nature, seasonal rhythms in diet & activity. Sunlight, fresh air = Natural healers. प्रकृति is your original home — return to it often.

PrakritiNatureSeasons
07
उद्देश्य

Purpose

Keeping the right intention, setting a goal. No purpose = No growth. उद्देश्य is the north star that makes every other Tatva meaningful and worth pursuing.

UddeshyaPurposeGoal
